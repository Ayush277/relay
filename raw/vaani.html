The Build Experience

It's even easier to build an agent now

Taking too long to build an agent from scratch?

No problem. Launching Teacher AI. Let our AI build it for you.

  1. Tough to build a nuanced agent? Not anymore

    Describe the agent in your own words

    Tell Teacher AI who to call, what to say, and what counts as success, in plain language. You don't need prompt engineering or flowcharts. One sentence is enough to start.

  2. Agents breaking on the questions nobody thought to ask?

    Answer a few clarifying questions

    Teacher AI asks about what's missing: languages, objections, qualifying criteria, and handoffs. Each answer tightens the agent before it ever makes a call.

  3. Stitching voice, language and tools together takes time?

    Watch it build in seconds

    The prompt, voice, language mix, and tools come together live, with a readiness score as it goes. You get a deployment-ready agent, not a draft to finish.

  4. One prompt tweak quietly breaks a live agent?

    Review the prompt diff, then accept

    See exactly what changed, with recommended settings for latency, voice, and turn-taking. Nothing is saved until you approve it.

  5. Every small change starting the build over?

    Keep refining by chat

    Ask Teacher AI to change the tone, add a language, or handle a new objection on an existing agent. It updates the same way, with a diff to review.

  6. That’s it. Your agent is ready for its first call.

Automated call screening leading to connectivity issues?

Solved!

A screening prompt is a step in the call. Vaani answers it and stays on the line while the customer decides whether to pick up, keeping the conversation ready for the handoff.

When asked who's calling, Vaani shares the agent's name, your company, and the reason for reaching out. The customer gets a clear introduction before answering, with enough context to understand why the call matters.

Once the customer picks up, Vaani introduces itself again and explains the purpose of the call. The conversation starts with a proper greeting, so the customer can follow along even if they missed the screening exchange.

About 98% of screening calls are handled in under 15 seconds. Vaani takes care of the brief exchange with the screener, then moves into the conversation when the customer accepts the call.
